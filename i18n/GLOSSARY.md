# Translation glossary

Canonical translations for VisaNav's domain terms. Every surface (Vue app, Astro
marketing, Django API) must render these the same way, so translators and the
machine-first pass start here rather than reinventing a term per catalog.

Rules:

- If a term appears below, use exactly this translation. Do not paraphrase.
- Proper nouns and agency names (USCIS, EOIR, DOJ, Form I-130) stay in English.
- Add a row before introducing a new domain term in any catalog.
- Keep tone plain and welcoming. Our audience includes people under stress reading
  in their second language.
