Form I-130, Petition for Alien Relative, is used by a U.S. citizen or lawful
permanent resident to establish a qualifying family relationship with a relative
who wants to immigrate to the United States. Filing and approval of an I-130 is
usually the first step; it does not by itself grant any immigration status or
benefit.

## Who files it

The petitioner (the U.S. citizen or permanent resident relative) files the
petition on behalf of the beneficiary (the relative who wishes to immigrate).

## What it does and does not do

- It establishes that a qualifying relationship exists.
- It does **not** grant a green card, work authorization, or permission to stay.
- The beneficiary still needs a visa to be available and must separately apply
  for permanent residence.

## Filing

The form can be filed online or by mail. Check the official I-130 page for the
current edition, filing fee, and mailing addresses before filing, since these
change over time.
